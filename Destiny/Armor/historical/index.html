Explanation

The What and the Why Review

What is this? This should be all the armor stats for the different armor mods you can run on your ghost, organized by class, for Zavala’s weekly armor offerings (Luminopotent and Triumphal Anthem) and for Xur’s weekly armor offerings (either Bushido or Swordmaster and Exotic Armor). As you know, changing the armor mod on your ghost can affect the stats of armor you can acquire.

Note: all of this is viewing the armor as Tier 5. Your stats will vary if you’re not acquiring Tier 5 yet.

Why is this? Well, sometimes I’m hunting for the best role that I’m missing. And man is it a hassle to switch the ghost mod and then recheck to see what the stats are now trying to note which one is the best.

Why JSON? I provide the JSON for those that want to do deeper analysis. For instance, you could load the JSON into an LLM and have it do deeper searching or comparing, perhaps, against your DIM exported armor.csv - it gives you flexibility.

As always, I’m open to Reddit post feedback / corrections / concerns, etc, etc. Thanks for the feedback that have made this better and better! Cheers from just another guardian.
